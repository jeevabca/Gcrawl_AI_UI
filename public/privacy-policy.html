Privacy Policy

We value your privacy. This policy explains how GcrawlAI collects, uses, and protects your information — and what we never do with it.

Last Revised: May 14, 2026 · Effective: May 14, 2026
Who We Are This Privacy Policy applies to GcrawlAI, a web crawling and data extraction API platform developed and operated by Gramosoft Private Limited (CIN: U72900TN2022PTC153168, GSTIN: 33AAJCG8548F1ZF), a company incorporated under the laws of India, with its registered office in Chennai, Tamil Nadu.

When we say "we," "us," or "our," we mean Gramosoft / GcrawlAI. When we say "you" or "your," we mean any individual or business accessing GcrawlAI's website at gcrawlai.com or using our API services.

This policy should be read alongside our Terms of Service. If you have any questions, reach us at privacy@gcrawlai.com.

1 What Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you register for GcrawlAI or use our Services, we collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and company name
  • Payment information — billing details processed via our third-party payment providers (Stripe / Razorpay). We do not store your full card details on our servers
  • Communications — messages or feedback you send us via email or support channels

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit gcrawlai.com or interact with our API, we automatically collect certain technical data:

  • IP address and approximate location (city/country level)
  • Browser type, version, and operating system
  • Pages visited, time and date of visit, and time spent on pages
  • Referring website or source
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
  • API call timestamps, endpoints accessed, HTTP status codes, and credit usage

This technical metadata is used solely for operational, billing, security, and rate-limiting purposes. It does not include the content of data you crawl via the API.

1.3 What We Do NOT Collect

✅ GcrawlAI Does Not Store Your Crawled Data GcrawlAI does not store, log, retain, or cache the HTML, text, or structured data retrieved from websites on your behalf. All crawled content is processed in real time and returned directly to you via the API response. Once delivered, no copy of your crawled data remains on GcrawlAI's servers.

2 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect only for the following purposes:

  • Providing the Services — to authenticate your account, process API requests, and deliver results
  • Billing and payments — to manage subscriptions, process charges, and issue GST invoices
  • Service communications — to notify you of service issues, downtime, new features, plan changes, or billing updates via email
  • Security and fraud prevention — to detect, investigate, and prevent unauthorised access or abuse of our platform
  • Platform improvement — to analyse usage patterns (in aggregate and anonymised form) to improve API performance and reliability
  • Legal compliance — to meet our obligations under applicable Indian and international laws

We do not use your data for advertising, behavioural profiling, or selling to third parties.

3 No Use of Data for AI or Machine Learning

✅ Your Data Is Never Used to Train AI Models GcrawlAI does not use any data collected through its APIs — including crawled content, API inputs, or outputs — to develop, train, fine-tune, or improve any artificial intelligence or machine learning models, whether our own or third-party. All data retrieved via our API is used solely to fulfil your request and is never retained, analysed, or repurposed.

4 How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share your data with third parties in the following limited circumstances:

Third Party Purpose Data Shared
Stripe / Razorpay Payment processing Name, email, billing details. Card data is stored by the processor, not by us.
Email / Communication tools Transactional emails (billing, support) Name and email address only
Analytics providers Website usage analytics (aggregated) Anonymised, non-identifying usage data
Legal / Regulatory authorities Compliance with court orders or legal obligations Only the minimum required; we notify you where permitted by law

All third-party service providers we work with are contractually bound to process your data only for the specified purpose and in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

5 Cookies and Tracking

5.1 What Are Cookies

Cookies are small data files stored on your browser when you visit a website. We use cookies to keep you logged in, understand how you use our website, and improve your experience.

5.2 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies — required for the website and dashboard to function (e.g. session management, authentication)
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand page views, navigation patterns, and feature usage in aggregated form
  • Preference cookies — remember your settings and preferences across visits

5.3 Your Cookie Choices

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to alert you when cookies are being sent. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of parts of our website. We respect Do Not Track (DNT) signals from browsers.

6 Data Security

We take data security seriously and implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • TLS/HTTPS encryption for all data in transit between your systems and GcrawlAI's servers
  • Encryption at rest for sensitive account and billing data
  • Access controls — your data is accessible only to personnel who need it to perform their duties, and they are bound by confidentiality obligations
  • Regular security reviews of our infrastructure and API endpoints
  • Stateless API processing — crawled content is never written to persistent storage

No system can guarantee 100% security. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and relevant regulatory authorities as required under applicable law, including India's DPDPA 2023.

7 Data Retention

We retain different categories of data for different periods:

  • Crawled / API output data — not retained at all; discarded immediately after the API response is delivered
  • Account information — retained for the duration of your account and for up to 3 years after account closure for legal and audit purposes
  • Billing and transaction records — retained for 7 years as required under Indian tax and accounting laws
  • API usage metadata (timestamps, credit usage, endpoints) — retained for up to 12 months for billing accuracy and dispute resolution
  • Support communications — retained for up to 2 years

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting privacy@gcrawlai.com. We will process your request within 30 days, subject to any legal retention obligations.

8 Where Your Data Is Stored

GcrawlAI's servers and infrastructure are primarily located in India. Some third-party service providers (such as payment processors and analytics tools) may process data in other countries.

Where data is transferred outside India, we ensure adequate protections are in place, including contractual safeguards compliant with applicable data protection laws. If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), such transfers will be subject to Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms.

9 Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Rights Available to You
  • Right to access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to correction — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention obligations
  • Right to restriction — request that we limit how we process your data
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object — object to processing of your data for direct marketing or automated profiling
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time

These rights apply to users in India (under DPDPA 2023), the EU/EEA (under GDPR), California (under CCPA), and other jurisdictions with applicable privacy laws. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@gcrawlai.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9.1 California Residents (CCPA)

California residents may request, up to twice per 12-month period, a summary of the personal information we hold. Upon written request, and absent a legal obligation to retain such information, we will delete your personal data. Requests should be submitted to privacy@gcrawlai.com.

9.2 EU / EEA Residents (GDPR)

If you are located in the EU or EEA, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority if you believe your data has been processed unlawfully. We are committed to resolving complaints directly wherever possible before escalation to a regulator.

9.3 Indian Residents (DPDPA 2023)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to access, correct, and erase your personal data, nominate a representative in the event of your incapacity, and grieve any violation of your data rights. Contact us at privacy@gcrawlai.com to exercise these rights.

10 Children's Privacy

GcrawlAI is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at privacy@gcrawlai.com and we will delete such information promptly.

11 Third-Party Links and Services

Our website and documentation may contain links to third-party websites or services. GcrawlAI is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party site you visit.

Our API infrastructure uses third-party proxy and network services solely to facilitate web crawling on your behalf. These providers do not have access to your account data or crawled content beyond what is technically necessary to route your request.

12 Do Not Track

Some browsers allow you to send a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal to websites. GcrawlAI respects DNT signals. When a DNT signal is detected, we limit our data collection to only what is strictly necessary to provide the service and will not use analytics cookies or behavioural tracking for that session.
